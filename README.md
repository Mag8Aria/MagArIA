MagArIA is an independent R&D and potential product initiative conceived by
**Aria Magrini**, exploring a local-first, open-by-design system for managing
creative production and organisational decisions. The project is informed by
the evolving **Controlled In(ter)ference** framework.

## Core Hypothesis

Selective cooperation between deterministic rules, open-weight LLMs,
specialised Decision Models and accountable human judgment *may* improve
decision quality and timeliness while reducing computational cost, energy use,
rework and invisible human labour.

These benefits are research questions, **not demonstrated outcomes**.

## Current Focus — P0

The initial phase addresses the transition from new opportunity to production:

- Structured intake
- Missing-data checks
- Constraints validation
- Recommendations
- Human review
